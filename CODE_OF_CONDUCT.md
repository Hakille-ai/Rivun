# Code of Conduct

rivun should be a place where serious technical work can happen without ego
getting in the way.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and precise.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, but respond quickly when harm is pointed out.
- Make space for newcomers who are learning Rust, networking, cryptography, or
  systems design.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberately derailing technical discussions.
- Pressuring maintainers to merge unsafe or unreviewed changes.
- Sharing exploit details publicly before maintainers have had a reasonable
  chance to respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or reject
contributions that violate this code. Serious security or abuse reports should
be handled privately where possible.
